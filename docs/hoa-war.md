## Cast
- **Tyrone Bellamy**, HOA President. Smooth, charming, a laminated vision board called "Maple Grove: Elevated". Fines
  you with a warm smile.
- **Sharique Bellamy**, Vice President and Architectural Review chair, Tyrone's husband. Deadpan, tape measure, leather
  planner. "I'm not mad. I'm measuring."
- They are a power couple who bicker lovingly mid-villainy. Their villainy is HOA pettiness and power. The comedy never
  comes from their race or sexuality.
- **Gary Kim**, Compliance Officer, their underling. Heavyset, very near-sighted, buried in forms, stamps everything in
  triplicate. The jokes are about paperwork and his eyesight, never his ethnicity (no accent gags, no caricature).
  Gary's own Final Notice mission and the bulldozer boss in haunted.js stay as they are.

## Chapter 1: Notice to Vacate (built, src/js/hoastory.js)
1. The couple serve a Notice to Vacate at the RV (first time the player walks up to it). Clock: 3 game days.
2. Every 8 AM Sharique pins a new violation: the fines go up.
3. Neighbors sign the petition once you've helped them: Nana, the Quik Stop Kids, Big Ricky, Rosie (Fish Order), Earl
   (Catch of the Day).
4. With 3 signatures the board sits on the HOA office steps 6 to 9 PM: a menu turn battle. Unpaid fines let Tyrone heal
   ("Outstanding Balance"), so money from odd jobs makes the fight easier. Lose: +$100, try again the next evening.
5. Clock runs out: the RV is booted (no interior, no nap/save) until the fines are paid at the HOA office door.
6. Win: MOTION DENIED, the eviction is suspended until the HOA election, +$250.

## Odd jobs (built, src/js/oddjobs.js)
Cork board by the RV: Pizza Run, Lawn Duty, Tow Rescue. Work rep raises pay; three in a row pays a bonus.
